How to play

Three rules. Endlessly addictive.

  1. Tap at the right moment

    The block slides back and forth. Tap, click or press space to drop it onto the tower.

  2. What sticks out, falls off

    Not quite precise? Then your block gets narrower. The narrower your tower, the harder it gets.

  3. Perfect = bonus

    Spot on? +2 points. Three perfects in a row and your block grows back a little.
