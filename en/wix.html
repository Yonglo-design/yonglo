Why Wix is the smart choice for your business website.

As a business you want a website that looks professional, works well and is easy to manage. Wix offers a strong foundation for that. With Yonglo you get the most out of it.

A professional website, fast

With Wix, a modern and professional website can be built efficiently. We tailor the design to your brand, your audience and your goals, so you're online quickly with a website that truly fits you.

Easy to manage yourself

A big advantage of Wix is that you can easily update your website yourself after launch. You change texts, photos, opening hours, services or blogs yourself, without technical knowledge.

Whether you're just starting out or running a growing business: Wix can grow with your ambitions. You can easily add pages, features and marketing tools such as online booking, a store, newsletters and social media later.
