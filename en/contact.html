GeneralWho do you work for?

Yonglo works for Dutch- and English-speaking businesses, from SMEs to multinationals. As a certified Wix Partner we build and optimise websites in Wix and Wix Studio. We also help with concepts, branding, marketing, SEO and custom development.

GeneralDo you have a lot of knowledge and experience?

Definitely. We've been working with Wix for more than 18 years as an official Wix Partner and were Alpha and Beta testers for Wix worldwide. Our team consists of web designers, developers and online specialists, with extensive experience in concepts, branding and marketing.

GeneralCan you also do custom coding?

Yes, through Yonglo Development. With Velo by Wix we build custom functionality, such as custom forms, integrations, databases and customer portals. Want to know whether your idea is possible? Feel free to get in touch.

GeneralWhat's the best way to contact you?

Email us at contact@yonglo.com or fill in the quote form on this page. During office hours (Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:30 CET) you can also reach us by phone and WhatsApp on +31 85 20 33 223.

WixI have a question about Wix itself. Can you help?

Yonglo is not an official Wix help desk. For general questions about the platform, such as the editor, billing or your subscription, the Wix Help Center is the best place to go. For everything related to the design, build and growth of your website, we're happy to help.
