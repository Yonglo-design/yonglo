### Smart Cookie Popup for Wix: GDPR Compliance

URL: https://www.yonglo.com/help-docs/smart-cookie-popup-for-wix/gdpr-compliance

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is an EU law designed to protect the privacy and personal data of individuals. It applies to any website that collects or processes personal data of visitors located in the European Union, regardless of where the website owner is based.

Cookies and similar technologies can be considered personal data when they are used for tracking, analytics, advertising, or personalization. Under GDPR, these types of cookies may only be placed after a visitor has given informed, explicit consent.

Smart Cookie Popup is built to help you meet these requirements, but proper configuration is essential to remain compliant.

> Always consult a legal professional in your jurisdiction to ensure your cookie consent setup meets all applicable legal requirements.

###### 1. Consent must be freely given and explicit

GDPR requires that consent is:

- Freely given (no pressure or forced agreement)
- Specific and informed (users understand what they are agreeing to)
- Unambiguous (a clear action is required)

This means users must be able to actively choose whether to accept or decline non‑essential cookies. Pre‑selected options, implied consent, or passive actions (such as continuing to browse) are not considered valid consent under GDPR.

A popup is commonly used because it clearly presents this choice before any cookies are placed.

###### 2. Button design and visual balance

The design of your cookie popup plays a critical role in GDPR compliance. Many EU regulators require that the Accept and Decline buttons have equal visual prominence. If one option is more noticeable, larger, brighter, or easier to click than the other, consent may be considered invalid.

Modifying the popup design—especially button size, color, placement, or contrast can therefore affect compliance.

Smart Cookie Popup includes a default “Equal Buttons” style, which ensures that both options are displayed with the same visual weight and emphasis.

Before customizing the design, always consult a legal professional in your jurisdiction to confirm that your changes align with local GDPR enforcement guidelines.

###### 3. Clear and honest consent text

The text shown in your cookie popup is just as important as its design. GDPR requires that users are clearly informed about:

- What types of cookies are used
- Why they are used
- Whether consent is optional
- 

Consent text must be easy to understand and free from misleading or manipulative language. Avoid wording that pressures users to accept cookies or suggests that accepting is required to use the website unless this is legally justified.

Changing the wording of the popup can directly affect the legal validity of the consent. Even small phrasing changes can alter how regulators interpret user intent.

###### 4. Responsibility and local legal differences

While GDPR is an EU‑wide regulation, its interpretation and enforcement can vary between countries and regulatory authorities. Industry‑specific rules and national guidance may also apply.

Smart Cookie Popup provides GDPR‑friendly defaults, but compliance ultimately depends on how the popup is configured and how cookies are implemented on your site.

###### 5. Ongoing compliance

GDPR compliance is not a one‑time task. Laws, regulatory guidance, and best practices evolve over time. Periodically review your popup design, text, and cookie usage to ensure continued compliance.

If you make changes to your website, add new tools, or introduce new tracking technologies, you may need to update your consent setup accordingly.

### Smart Cookie Popup for Wix: Setting up Managed Google Tags

URL: https://www.yonglo.com/help-docs/smart-cookie-popup-for-wix/setting-up-managed-google-tags

Managed Google Tags lets Smart Cookie Popup load your Google tags and manage Google Consent Mode v2 for you. Turn it on only if you want this app, not Wix Marketing Integrations or Custom Code, to load Google Tag Manager or direct Google measurement IDs on your site. This feature is optional, so if you only want a cookie popup you can skip it.

###### What Managed Google Tags does

- Loads your Google tags only after consent is handled correctly
- Manages Google Consent Mode v2 for you automatically
- Works with Google Tag Manager, direct Google tag IDs, or both

###### When to use it

Use Managed Google Tags if you want this app to be the single place that loads your Google tags. Leave it off if you do not use Google tags, or if you prefer to manage them somewhere else.

###### Where to find it

- Open Smart Cookie Popup from your dashboard.
- Go to the Design tab.
- Scroll to Managed Google Tags.
- Check Enable managed Google tags to open the 3-step setup guide.

You will see a status badge next to the feature: Not enabled, Setup incomplete, or Ready. Remember to click Save Changes and activate so your settings go live.

###### Step 1: Add the Google IDs this app should load

Add the IDs you want this app to load. You can use a Google Tag Manager container, direct Google tag IDs, or both.

- Google Tag Manager ID (optional): one container in the format GTM-XXXXXXX.
- Direct Google tag IDs (optional): one per line. Use G- for GA4, AW- for Google Ads, and DC- for Floodlight.

You need at least one valid ID (a GTM container, one or more direct IDs, or both).

Use GTM if your tags are managed in Google Tag Manager. Use direct IDs if you load GA4, Google Ads, or Floodlight without GTM. If you leave GTM empty, only your direct tags will load.

Warning: If you use both GTM and direct IDs, make sure the direct IDs do not also fire from inside your GTM container. Loading the same ID in two places can count your visits and conversions twice.

###### Step 2: Check for duplicate Google installs

Make sure these Google IDs are not already installed anywhere else on this site. Many sites have no Google tags installed yet, so if that is you, this step is quick. If you are not sure, check these places:

- Wix Marketing Integrations: disconnect Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, or related Google integrations that load the same IDs.
- Settings, then Custom Code: remove any Google snippets for the same IDs.
- Other apps, embedded HTML, or custom elements that may add Google scripts.

Why it matters: duplicate installs can make Consent Mode start too late, and your visits and conversions may be counted twice.

When you have checked, tick the box: I confirmed there are no duplicate Google installs for these IDs.

###### Step 3: Save and verify

- Click Save Changes in the app and activate.
- Visit your live site or a preview.
- Accept cookies so the app loads your tags. Tags load after consent.
- Verify your tags are firing.

To verify GTM, use GTM Preview mode. Avoid the basic Test your website option, because it often misses tags that load through a cookie consent app.

To verify direct GA4, Google Ads, or Floodlight tags, use Google Tag Assistant after you accept cookies.

When setup is complete you will see: Consent defaults will run before this app loads your Google tags. The status badge will change to Ready.

##### FAQ

**Do I need Managed Google Tags if I only want a cookie popup?** No. Managed Google Tags is optional. If you only want a cookie consent popup, you can leave it off. Turn it on only if you want this app to load your Google tags and manage Consent Mode for you.

**What ID formats does the app accept?** Use GTM-XXXXXXX for a Google Tag Manager container, G- for GA4, AW- for Google Ads, and DC- for Floodlight. You need at least one valid ID, and you can add several direct IDs one per line.

**Can I use both GTM and direct IDs?** Yes, but each Google ID should load in only one place. If the same ID fires both as a direct ID and from inside your GTM container, you may see double counting. Keep each ID in either GTM or the direct list.

### Smart Cookie Popup for Wix: Using Google Tag Manager with Smart Cookie Popup

URL: https://www.yonglo.com/help-docs/smart-cookie-popup-for-wix/using-google-tag-manager-with-smart-cookie-popup

If your Google tags are managed in Google Tag Manager, you can let Smart Cookie Popup load your GTM container after consent and manage Consent Mode v2 for you. You only need one container ID, and you do not add any code yourself.

###### When to use GTM

Choose GTM if you already manage GA4, Google Ads, or other tags inside a Google Tag Manager container. With this setup, the app loads your container and handles consent for you.

###### Where to find your GTM ID

- Open Google Tag Manager and select your account and container.
- Look at the top of the workspace for your container ID.
- It looks like GTM-XXXXXXX. Copy it.

Add this single ID to the Google Tag Manager ID field in Step 1 of the setup guide.

###### Remove old GTM installs from Wix

Before you go live, make sure the same container is not already loading somewhere else on your site. If it is, your tags can load twice. Check these places:

- Wix Marketing Integrations: disconnect any Google Tag Manager connection that uses the same container.
- Settings, then Custom Code: remove GTM snippets for the same container.
- Other apps or embedded code that may add GTM.

###### Verify with GTM Preview mode

- Save and activate your settings in the app.
- Open your live site or a preview and accept cookies.
- In Google Tag Manager, click Preview and connect to your site.
- Confirm your tags fire after consent is given.

Tip: Use GTM Preview mode rather than the basic Test your website check.

###### Why Test your website may show false negatives

The Test your website tool often loads before consent is handled, so it can miss tags that a cookie consent app loads after the visitor chooses. That does not mean your tags are broken. GTM Preview mode gives you a more accurate picture, because you can accept cookies and then watch the tags fire.

##### FAQ

**Do I still need a cookie popup if I use GTM?** Yes. The cookie popup is what asks visitors for consent and records their choice. Managed Google Tags then loads your GTM container based on that choice. The popup and GTM work together.

**Why does GTM Test your website say my tags are missing?** That tool often loads before consent is handled, so it can miss tags that load through a cookie consent app. Use GTM Preview mode instead, accept cookies, and then watch your tags fire.

**How do I add Google Tag Manager to a Wix site with cookie consent?** Enable Managed Google Tags in Smart Cookie Popup, open the Design tab, and paste your GTM-XXXXXXX container ID into the Google Tag Manager ID field in Step 1. The app loads your container after visitors give consent, so you do not need to add GTM code in Wix Custom Code.

### Smart Cookie Popup for Wix: Avoiding duplicate Google installs

URL: https://www.yonglo.com/help-docs/smart-cookie-popup-for-wix/avoiding-duplicate-google-installs

For Managed Google Tags to work correctly, this app should be the only place that loads your Google IDs. If the same ID also loads somewhere else, you can run into double counting and consent timing problems. This article shows where to check.

###### Checklist: where the same ID might be hiding

- Wix Marketing Integrations: Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, or related Google integrations.
- Settings, then Custom Code: Google snippets added by you or a previous setup.
- Other apps that connect Google Analytics or Ads.
- Embedded HTML or custom elements that include Google scripts.

###### Symptoms of a duplicate install

- Double pageviews in Google Analytics.
- Conversion counts that look too high in Google Ads.
- Tags that appear to fire before a visitor has made a choice.

###### How to fix it

- Decide that this app will be the single loader for each Google ID.
- Disconnect the same IDs from Wix Marketing Integrations.
- Remove matching Google snippets from Settings, then Custom Code.
- Check other apps and embedded code for the same IDs and remove them.
- Tick the confirmation box in Step 2, then save and verify.

Important: Using the same ID in two places is a problem even if you think only one is active. Make sure each Google ID loads in just one place.

##### FAQ

**Can I keep Google Analytics connected in Wix Marketing Integrations?** Not for the same IDs that this app loads. If this app loads a Google ID, that same ID should not also load through Wix Marketing Integrations, or your visits and conversions may be counted twice. Disconnect the matching Google integration in Marketing Integrations.

**Why must this app be the only Google tag loader?** So consent is handled before your tags load, and so each visit and conversion is counted once. If the same ID loads from another place, Consent Mode can start too late and your data can be doubled.

**Why am I seeing double pageviews or doubled conversions on my Wix site?** Double counting usually means the same Google ID is loading in more than one place. Check Wix Marketing Integrations, Settings then Custom Code, and any other apps for the same ID, then remove the duplicates so Smart Cookie Popup is the only loader for that ID.

### Smart Cookie Popup for Wix: Google Consent Mode and cookie categories

URL: https://www.yonglo.com/help-docs/smart-cookie-popup-for-wix/google-consent-mode-and-cookie-categories

When Managed Google Tags is enabled and set up, the app connects your visitors’ cookie choices to Google Consent Mode v2. You do not need to add any Consent Mode code yourself. This article explains how it works.

###### How visitor choices map to Google

The app maps your Wix cookie categories to Google Consent Mode signals like this:

- Analytics cookies control analytics_storage.
- Marketing cookies control ad_storage.
- Marketing plus third-party data sharing control ad_user_data and ad_personalization.
- Functional cookies control functionality_storage and personalization_storage.
- Essential and security are always allowed.

###### What happens on Accept, Decline, or Customize

- New visitors: consent starts as denied until the visitor makes a choice.
- Returning visitors: their saved choice is restored, so tags do not reset incorrectly.
- When a visitor changes preferences through Accept, Decline, Customize, or Save, consent updates automatically.

###### You do not need to add Consent Mode code

The app handles Consent Mode v2 for you when Managed Google Tags is set up correctly. There is no need to paste any code into Custom Code.

Please note: This app helps support your compliance efforts, but it does not provide legal advice or guarantee compliance. For questions about your obligations, consult a legal professional.

##### FAQ

**Does this replace Wix consent logging?** No. Smart Cookie Popup still works with Wix native consent policy system and logs consent in the Wix Privacy Center. Managed Google Tags adds the loading of your Google tags and Consent Mode v2 on top of that.

**What is Google Consent Mode v2 and do I need it on my Wix site?** Google Consent Mode v2 tells Google how to behave based on each visitor cookie choice. If you run Google Analytics or Google Ads for visitors in the EU or UK, you generally need it. When Managed Google Tags is set up, Smart Cookie Popup applies Consent Mode v2 for you automatically.

**How does Smart Cookie Popup connect cookie consent to my Google tags?** It maps your Wix cookie categories to Google Consent Mode signals. Analytics cookies control analytics_storage, marketing cookies control ad_storage, and marketing with third-party data sharing controls ad_user_data and ad_personalization. Essential cookies are always allowed.

### Smart Cookie Popup for Wix: Troubleshooting Managed Google Tags

URL: https://www.yonglo.com/help-docs/smart-cookie-popup-for-wix/troubleshooting-managed-google-tags

If your tags are not behaving the way you expect, work through these common situations. Most issues come from setup that is not finished, or from the same Google ID loading in more than one place.

###### Setup is stuck on Setup incomplete

- Make sure you added at least one valid ID in Step 1 (a GTM container, one or more direct IDs, or both).
- Check that each ID uses the right format: GTM-, G-, AW-, or DC-.
- Tick the confirmation box in Step 2.
- Click Save Changes and activate.

###### Tags are not firing

- Confirm you accepted cookies on the site. Tags load after consent, so nothing fires before a choice is made.
- Check that the ID is entered correctly with no extra spaces.
- Make sure the same ID was not disabled or removed elsewhere by mistake.
- Save and activate again, then reload the live site.

###### GTM Preview will not connect

- Open your live site or preview in the same browser.
- Accept cookies first, then start GTM Preview.
- Use GTM Preview mode rather than the basic Test your website option, which can miss consent-managed tags.

###### Tag Assistant shows nothing until I accept cookies

This is expected. The app loads your direct tags only after consent, so Tag Assistant stays empty until you accept cookies. Accept cookies and the tags should appear.

###### Using both GTM and direct IDs safely

You can use a GTM container and direct IDs together, but each Google ID should load in only one place. If an ID fires both directly and from inside GTM, you may see double counting. Keep each ID in either GTM or the direct list, not both.

##### FAQ

**Why do not my tags show before I accept cookies?** This is expected behavior. The app loads your Google tags only after a visitor accepts cookies, so nothing fires beforehand. Accept cookies on the site and your tags should appear in GTM Preview or Tag Assistant.

**My Google tags are not firing on my Wix site, how do I fix it?** First accept cookies on the live site, because tags load only after consent. Then check that your ID is entered correctly with no spaces, that the format is valid (GTM-, G-, AW-, or DC-), and that you clicked Save Changes and activated. Reload the live site and test again with GTM Preview or Tag Assistant.

**Why does my Managed Google Tags setup say Setup incomplete?** Setup stays incomplete until you add at least one valid Google ID in Step 1, tick the no-duplicates confirmation box in Step 2, and click Save Changes. Make sure each ID uses a valid format: GTM-, G-, AW-, or DC-.
